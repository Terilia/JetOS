Concept: left status MFD

Airframe first, systems underneath.

This keeps the top damage drawing as the visual anchor, then replaces the stacked cards with a compact synoptic layout: propulsion, fuel, and power all sit on one disciplined grid. It should feel more like a fighter systems page than a dashboard.

No card stack Thin dividers and meter rails replace boxed cards, so the screen feels lighter and less UI-kit.
Engine counts make sense USE is what thrust control drives. ALL is what the ship recognizes, including reverse-facing engines.
Debuggable at a glance Left/right engine counts, AB readiness, fuel thresholds, battery drain, and block health live in stable positions.
