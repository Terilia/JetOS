[03] Interactive Demos
See It Run
Browser-native recreations of JetOS subsystems. Play with the controls and watch the same algorithms that run inside Space Engineers.

HUD/HorizonRenderer.cs

Artificial Horizon

Pitch ladder with 5° increments — solid lines for nose-up, dashed for nose-down. The horizon line, FPM, and pitch markers all rotate around the cockpit center by -roll. Bank arc and aircraft symbol stay fixed.

Slide pitch and roll, or run the preset Split-S, Immelmann, and barrel roll maneuvers.

Modules/HUDModule.cs · UpdateThrottleControl()

Throttle & Equalization

Drag the W slider. Throttle clamps at 80% (MIL) until the AB gate is passed — release W and re-press, or hold for 40 ticks. Then hydrogen tanks unlock and afterburner engages.

Click the damage scenarios to see equalization rebalance the override percentages. The yaw torque indicator stays balanced even when one side is at 50% capacity.

HUD/RadarRenderer.cs

Auto-Scaling Radar

Smoothed range tracker — display range follows the farthest contact times 1.30 padding, smoothed at α=0.10 so the scope doesn't twitch. Floor of 2000m.

Click Cycle Target to step through contacts sorted by distance (the same logic that FlipGPS uses on numpad 8). Color-coded by closing speed and time-to-intercept.

Utilities/SoundManager.cs

Priority + State Machine

Two channels (warning and weapon) each run a 4-state machine: IDLE → STOPPING → SELECTING → PLAYING with a 3-frame delay between each transition. This is what survives Space Engineers' double-Main()-per-tick problem.

Click priority buttons to fire requests. The highest priority per channel wins; lower-priority requests are silently dropped that tick.

[ OPEN FULLSCREEN ]

Configuration → HUD Theme

Color Themes

Four built-in themes: Green (default), Cyan, Amber, White. Theme is stored as hud_theme=0..3 and cached once per frame so all renderers stay in sync without per-call lookups.

Click each tab to swap palettes — the artificial horizon, radar, and instruments all update together.

UI/StatusPanelRenderer.cs

Live Engine Schematic

1:1 JavaScript port of the in-game MFD propulsion display. 3D-projected compressor blade discs, 48 golden-ratio-spaced air particles per engine, multi-tongue exhaust plume that reflects throttle stage, and resource cards.

Throttle cycles automatically through IDLE, NORMAL, MIL, and AB.
